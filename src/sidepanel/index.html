What results mean

Pass means the assessed criteria were met; Warning means an issue or improvement needs review; Fail means the available evidence supports a problem. Not Evaluated means there is not enough reliable evidence, while Not Applicable means the check does not apply; neither counts as a pass or fail.

Metadata and supported schema checks form the core. Rendered observations are best-effort. Comparisons require aligned sources; undetected content is not confirmed absent.

        Only explicit analysis reads this page. Results remain in session memory (up to 10 reports, 30 minutes); preferences and aggregate counters remain locally until cleared. No browsing history, form values, personal accounts or page data are sent to Magenable or third parties.
